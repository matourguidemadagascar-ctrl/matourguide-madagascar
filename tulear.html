Gateway to the Great South

Where red rock meets the Mozambique Channel

Tuléar is a region of extremes — arid canyons carved over millions of years, spiny forests found nowhere else on Earth, and a coastline shaped by the Vezo fishing communities who have sailed these waters for generations. It's a landscape as varied as it is unforgettable.
