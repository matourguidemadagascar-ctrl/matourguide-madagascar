    Morondava – Kirindy – Tsingy de Bemaraha Circuit

    4 days / 3 nights in Bekopaka

    Day 1
    Morondava → Kirindy → Bekopaka

    05:30 departure from Menabe Morondava hotel. Guided visit of Kirindy Reserve. Tsiribihina and Manambolo ferry crossings. Overnight at Tanankoy Bekopaka.

    Day 2
    Petit Tsingy

    Guided excursion to the Petit Tsingy. Canoe trip on the Manambolo River. Overnight at Tanankoy Bekopaka.

    Day 3
    Grand Tsingy (UNESCO)

    Full day exploring the Grand Tsingy de Bemaraha. Overnight at Tanankoy Bekopaka.

    Day 4
    Bekopaka → Morondava

    Return via ferries. Stop at the Avenue of the Baobabs and the Baobab Amoureux.

    🌳 Kirindy Reserve 🦎 Endemic wildlife 🚣 Manambolo River canoe ⛰️ Petit Tsingy 🏔️ Grand Tsingy (UNESCO) 🌅 Avenue of the Baobabs ❤️ Baobab Amoureux

    Included

    • Private vehicle with driver + fuel
    • Ferry crossings
    • Entrance fees and local guides
    • Canoe trip
    • Accommodation in Bekopaka (3 nights)

    Not included

    • Meals and drinks
    • Personal expenses
    • Tips
    • Overnight in Morondava

    ⚠️ Day 1 has an early 05:30 departure. Day 3 requires good physical condition.
