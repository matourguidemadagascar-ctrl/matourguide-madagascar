A journey between baobabs, wildlife & stone cathedrals

Morondava is one of Madagascar's most iconic destinations. Its giant baobabs, untouched dry forests, spectacular limestone formations and peaceful rivers create unforgettable adventures for nature lovers, photographers and explorers.
